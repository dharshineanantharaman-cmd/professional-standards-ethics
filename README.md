# Professional Standards & Ethics

## Assessment

This assessment examines professional standards and ethical obligations in legal practice through two case studies. It considers potential breaches of professional duties by solicitors, including misleading the court, integrity, public confidence, conflicts of interest and the likely professional consequences of misconduct.

The assessment also includes a reflective portfolio exploring ten areas of professional ethics, including ethical theories, access to justice, regulatory bodies, lawyer-client relationships, confidentiality, conflicts of interest, fees, business ethics, alternative dispute resolution and litigation. It reflects on how these areas informed my professional development and approach to ethical decision-making.

## Abstract

This portfolio examines professional responsibility within legal practice, including integrity, duties to the court, confidentiality, conflicts of interest, professional misconduct and regulatory standards. It considers how ethical judgment and professional obligations shape responsible legal practice.

**Academic context:** LLB (Hons), University of East London.
